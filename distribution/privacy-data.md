# Privacy and data declarations

## Google Play Data Safety

- Data collection: none by the developer.
- Data sharing: none by the developer.
- Messages and provider credentials are sent only to the AI endpoint configured by the user.
- Native chat history and credentials remain on the user's device.

## App Store App Privacy

- Data linked to the user: none.
- Data used to track the user: none.
- Developer analytics and advertising: none.

## Platform limitations

- Web chat history and provider credentials are session-only.
- Apple Foundation Models require iOS 26+ or supported Apple Silicon macOS 26+ systems.
- Android on-device inference depends on compatible hardware and model availability.
- Desktop shell tools are constrained and unavailable on Android, iOS, and Web.
