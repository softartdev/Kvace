## Full description

Kvace is a local-first AI agent workspace for keeping conversations, configuring providers, and working with tools in one adaptive Kotlin Multiplatform app.

Use a local Ollama server, connect an OpenAI-compatible provider with your own credentials, or use supported on-device models. Your conversation history stays on your device on native platforms, while the web version keeps it only for the current browser session.

Kvace gives you clear provider settings, persistent local chat history, streaming responses, cancellation, and a configurable harness prompt. You choose the model endpoint and stay in control of your data.

## Release notes 1.0.0

First public release of Kvace: a local-first multiplatform AI agent workspace with local conversation history, Ollama, OpenAI-compatible providers, streaming responses, and adaptive Android, iOS, Desktop, and Web interfaces.

## App Review notes

Kvace has no account, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases. The app connects only to an AI provider selected and configured by the user. Local Ollama connections may use HTTP on the user's local network. Review the populated demo content without entering credentials.
